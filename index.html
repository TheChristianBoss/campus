Chicken & Potato Soup

My special soup starts with homemade chicken stock. After straining the stock, I add chicken breast, potatoes, carrots, onion, and lemon pepper.

See the full recipe

How I make it

1. Make the chicken stock

Boil chicken bones, diced chicken, chopped vegetables, and seasonings in water until the broth is full of flavor. Then strain it.

2. Make the soup

Use that stock to cook two pounds of chicken breast, five pounds of potatoes, two cans of carrots, onion, and lemon pepper.
