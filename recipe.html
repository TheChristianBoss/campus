My Soup Recipe

This recipe has two parts: first make the chicken stock, then use it to make the soup.

Ingredients

For the chicken stock

  • Raw chicken bones
  • Water (enough to cover the ingredients)
  • Chopped onions
  • Chopped bell peppers
  • Chopped carrots
  • About 1 pound of diced chicken meat or chicken breast
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Other seasonings, to taste

For the soup

  • The homemade chicken stock
  • 2 pounds of diced chicken breast
  • 5 pounds of diced potatoes
  • 2 cans of carrots
  • Chopped red or white onion
  • Lemon pepper seasoning, to taste

Instructions

Part 1: Make the stock

  1. Put raw chicken bones, about 1 pound of diced chicken, chopped onions, bell peppers, and carrots into a large pot.
  2. Add enough water to cover everything. Season with salt, pepper, and anything else you like.
  3. Bring to a boil, then simmer until the chicken is cooked and the broth has plenty of flavor.
  4. Carefully strain the pot into a clean container. Keep the liquid stock and remove the bones and solids.

Part 2: Make the soup

  1. Pour the strained stock into a large soup pot.
  2. Add 2 pounds of diced chicken breast, 5 pounds of diced potatoes, 2 cans of carrots, and some chopped red or white onion.
  3. Add a little lemon pepper seasoning to taste.
  4. Bring to a boil and simmer until the potatoes are tender and the chicken is fully cooked. Taste, adjust the seasoning, and serve.

Food safety tip

Use a food thermometer to make sure the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C). Refrigerate leftovers promptly.

Source: FDA safe food handling guidance.
