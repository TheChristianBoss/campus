About My Special Soup

This website is about how I make my chicken and potato soup from scratch.

What makes it special?

I start by making chicken stock with raw chicken bones, chicken meat, vegetables, and seasonings. I let everything cook until the flavor comes out, then strain the stock.

Next, I add diced chicken breast, potatoes, canned carrots, onion, and lemon pepper. It's a hearty soup with simple ingredients.
